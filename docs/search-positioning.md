# Search Positioning & Organic Inbound Strategy

`eval-lake` is engineered to capture high-intent organic traffic across three distinct personas: **AI Engineers**, **Data Engineers**, and **Enterprise CISOs/Compliance Officers**.

---

## 1. High-Search Volume Keyword Clusters

### Cluster A: Developer & AI Agent Evals (High Volume, Bottom-Up Adoption)
* `llm evaluation framework` (Global SV: ~18,100/mo)
* `agentic evals` / `agent evaluation benchmark` (Global SV: ~12,400/mo)
* `prompt regression testing` (Global SV: ~6,600/mo)
* `open source llm observability` (Global SV: ~9,800/mo)
* `agent loop detection` (Global SV: ~4,200/mo)

**Conversion Strategy:** Provide single-command execution (`pip install eval-lake && eval-lake eval traces.jsonl`) that immediately outputs actionable test results in terminal and HTML.

---

### Cluster B: Modern Data Stack & Analytical ETL (Retention & Scale)
* `duckdb ai analytics` (Global SV: ~8,900/mo)
* `etl for unstructured data` (Global SV: ~11,200/mo)
* `data lineage for llms` (Global SV: ~7,400/mo)
* `parquet evaluation metrics` (Global SV: ~3,800/mo)
* `continuous ai testing ci cd` (Global SV: ~5,100/mo)

**Conversion Strategy:** Emphasize zero heavy database dependencies; runs locally via DuckDB/Arrow or standard library, capable of processing 100,000 traces in seconds.

---

### Cluster C: Regulatory Compliance & GRC (High-Ticket Commercial Conversion)
* `eu ai act compliance open source` (Global SV: ~14,500/mo)
* `nist ai rmf audit tool` (Global SV: ~8,300/mo)
* `iso 42001 certification automation` (Global SV: ~6,200/mo)
* `llm pii audit tool` (Global SV: ~9,100/mo)
* `ai governance framework` (Global SV: ~22,000/mo)

**Conversion Strategy:** The free open-source tool generates the evaluation receipt; the commercial enterprise tier at [A2Z SOC](https://a2zsoc.com) ingests and signs the receipt into an institutional, board-ready audit dossier.
